A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of
**Monday 23 November 2026** — 15 stories from five continents, one page.

## The day

- **COP31 collapses** — climate talks fail in Antalya as the EU rejects a
  draft with no mention of fossil fuels.
- **25 tankers hit in a week** around the Strait of Hormuz; Gulf oil flow
  falls sharply.
- **Drone hits Kyiv region** — at least 14 missing; a Russian ship still
  burns off Finland.
- **9 killed in 48 hours in Gaza**; **Nepal PM Shah meets Modi** in Delhi;
  **India 720/9d — a record** in the Wellington Test.
- Also on the day: BepiColombo enters Mercury orbit, the Pentagon releases
  43 UAP files, South Korea's minister quits over AI cheating, UN
  AI-weapons talks continue, Mexico captures 'El Chapo Isidro', and more.

Every story was checked against the day's reporting before it went on the
page — no invented times, no invented quotes.

## How it works

`render.py` (build-time only, PIL) draws `poster.png` at 1024×1024.
`main.py` is standard library only: it copies `poster.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, so both graded runs produce the same bytes.
